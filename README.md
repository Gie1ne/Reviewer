⚡ Applied Electricity - Interactive Exam Reviewer
An interactive, single-page web application designed for students studying Applied Electricity and Analog Multimeter Diagnostics. It incorporates realistic visual identification, callout targets, interactive teardowns, flash-quizzes, a timed master exam with printable certificates, and an arcade matching game.
🌟 Key Features & Modules
1. 📦 Component Explorer & Study Cards
20 Electronic Devices & 14 Multimeter Parts: Full database with definitions, aliases, and operational safety rules.
Visual Callout Targets: HD realistic visual previews paired with high-contrast glowing callout target arrows (TARGET PART).
Real-Time Search & Filtering: Instant search across titles, descriptions, and rules; category filtering for Controls & Safety, Power & Loads, Multimeter Parts, and Wiring.
Grid / Table Views: Toggle between visual card grids and compact table layouts.
2. 🎛️ Sanwa Analog Multimeter (VOM) Interactive Teardown
Interactive Hotspot SVG Diagram: 14 clickable target locations covering all major parts of a Sanwa YX360TRF analog multimeter.
Guided Inspection Tour: Step-by-step tour walking through the panel, scale arches, mirror strip, zero position adjusters, range selector zones, and finger guards.
Clinical Operational Rules: Specific guidance on parallax error prevention, series vs. parallel connection rules, zero-ohm calibration, and de-energized testing safety.
3. 👁️ Visual Identification Flash-Quiz
High-Precision Image Callouts: Directs students to identify sub-components and devices indicated by animated callout target arrows.
Interactive Streak System: Tracks correct answer streaks and provides immediate feedback with detailed rule explanations upon choice selection.
4. 🎓 Master Assessment Exam Mode
25-Question Comprehensive Test: Covers device functions, multimeter operation, connection topologies (series/parallel), and color standards.
20-Minute Live Timer & Question Palette: Full exam interface with real-time progress bar, question status palette, and answer review options.
Rank Achievement System:
🏅 Master Electrician Rank: 90% – 100%
🥈 Journeyman Technician Rank: 75% – 89%
⚠️ Apprentice Level: Below 75%
Printable Certificate Generator: Generates an official digital Certificate of Mastery complete with student name, score, date, and rank.
5. 🧩 Circuit Wire & Symbol Matching Arcade
Interactive Card Matching Game: Tests rapid recall by pairing device terms with their core functions and operational rules.
Score Multiplier & Move Counter: Tracks total moves and matched pairs.
🛠️ Built With
HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript (ES6): Clean, lightweight single-file implementation.
Tailwind CSS: Modern cyber-styled UI, dark mode aesthetics, and responsive layout grid.
HTML5 Canvas API: Top header with animated high-voltage spark effects.
Web Audio API: Built-in sound synthesizer for click zaps, correct chimes, and wrong answer buzzes (toggleable).
FontAwesome & Google Fonts: Icons, Orbitron, and Inter typography.
